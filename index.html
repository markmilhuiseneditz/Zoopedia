1. Introduction to Superposition

In quantum mechanics, superposition is a fundamental principle stating that any two quantum states can be added together ("superposed") to result in another valid quantum state. Conversely, every quantum state can be represented as a sum of two or more other distinct states. When dealing with qubits, this means the system can exist in a state of 0, 1, or both simultaneously until observed.

2. Entanglement in Artificial Neural Networks

Quantum entanglement occurs when interacting particles yield a state that cannot be described independently of the state of the others. In modern Artificial Intelligence, researchers are exploring "Quantum Neural Networks" (QNNs) which mimic this behavior to solve complex optimization problems far faster than classical computers.
